          AI automation
          
LeadPilot

          
AI lead qualification for small businesses: every website inquiry is scored, summarized and answered before the owner opens their inbox.

          
    
            
  • Scores each lead hot, warm or cold with the reason, budget and urgency, and drafts a reply in the customer's language: English, French or Arabic.
    • 
            
  • Treats form messages as untrusted input: attempts to trick the AI are caught and filed as spam.
    • 
            
  • Sends every lead to n8n, Google Sheets or a CRM through a signed webhook, and keeps working offline when the AI is unavailable.
    •
